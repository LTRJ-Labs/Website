Project Overview

The MothNode system is a flagship internal research and development initiative at LTRJ Labs. Designed to address the challenges of tracking water resources in rural and off-grid environments, this system eliminates the need for manual inspections by delivering real-time telemetry from remote storage tanks directly to a central cloud platform.

By employing energy-efficient MCU architectures combined with long-range sub-GHz wireless transmitters (such as LoRa) and cellular modems, the device monitors hydrostatic pressure, temperature, and system battery levels. This project is currently in the prototyping phase and represents a key step toward launching our own commercial hardware product and subscription services.

Key Features

  • Long-Range Wireless Telemetry: Integrates sub-GHz wireless transceivers to transmit data reliably across several kilometers in rural terrain.
  • Ultra-Low Power Operations: Engineered to run on solar-charged lithium battery configurations, achieving multi-year operational cycles without manual recharging.
  • High-Precision Liquid Level Sensing: Employs temperature-compensated hydrostatic pressure sensors to log micro-changes in water volume.
  • Cloud Alert Dashboard: Feeds telemetry into an online portal configured to dispatch email and SMS notifications when water levels drop below key thresholds.
