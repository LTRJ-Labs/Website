Project Overview

Our team designed and routed a custom breakout board for Pink Pigeon Innovations to interface with their main PCB system. The breakout board serves as a vital diagnostic interface, allowing engineers to easily inspect, tap, and analyze signal traces in real-time during laboratory testing phases.

By routing core communication signals and power rails out to dedicated headers and probe points, the breakout board eliminates the risk of short-circuits or connection errors when attaching logic analyzers, oscilloscopes, or signal injectors directly to the main PCB.

Key Features

  • Diagnostic Test Points: Integrated easy-to-use solder loops and hook-style probe points for clean, noise-free scope measurements.
  • Robust Signal Integrity: Designed with matched impedance traces and optimal spacing to avoid cross-talk on high-speed lines.
  • Standardized Pin Headers: Breaks out all micro-pins to standard 0.1" (2.54mm) headers for immediate compatibility with breadboards and jumper wires.
  • Compact Form Factor: Engineered to minimize workspace footprint while maintaining sufficient physical space for testing probes.
Status: Completed & Delivered
The custom breakout PCB was fabricated, assembled, and fully verified. It is currently deployed and actively utilized for system debugging at Pink Pigeon Innovations.
