At LTRJ Labs Ltd ("we", "our", or "us"), we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and safeguard your personal information when you visit our website and contact our team.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when requesting consultations or contacting our team via email. This information may include:

  • Contact Details: Your name, email address, and phone number.
  • Inquiry Details: The topic of your inquiry, company details, and any message or files you submit when you email us directly.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected exclusively for professional consultation and client communication. Specifically, to:

  • Respond to your hardware design queries and project consultation requests.
  • Provide project quotes, schedule meetings, and follow up on business inquiries.
  • Comply with necessary engineering NDA standards and legal verification procedures.

3. Data Security and Protection

We implement standard industry security practices to safeguard your data against unauthorized access, loss, or disclosure. Because our website functions as a secure informational portfolio, we do not host user accounts, passwords, or credit card databases directly on this site, greatly minimizing potential security risks.

4. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or distribute your personal details to third-party marketing companies under any circumstances. We only share information with partners when necessary to fulfill your hardware project requirements (with your explicit, prior consent) or if legally obligated by law.

5. Your Rights and Deletion

You have the right to request access to the information we hold about you or request that we delete your contact records from our communication databases. If you wish to make a request, please contact us directly at our email address or submit a request through our contact page.

6. Contact Information

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or our data handling practices, please feel free to reach out to us via our Contact Page.
