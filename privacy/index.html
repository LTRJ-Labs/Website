At LTRJ Labs Ltd ("we", "our", or "us"), we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and safeguard your personal information when you visit our website and contact our team.

1. Personal Information You Provide

We do not use contact forms or input fields on our website, so our website does not directly collect, store, or process any personal details (such as your name, email address, or phone number) through form submissions. The only personal information we receive is what you voluntarily choose to provide to us when you contact our team directly via email (using your own email client or copying our address). This information may include:

  • Contact Details: Your name, email address, and phone number.
  • Inquiry Details: The topic of your inquiry, company details, and any message or files you submit when you email us.

2. Automated Data Collection (Cookies & Analytics)

When you visit our website, we use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to collect aggregated, non-personally identifiable information to help us understand visitor behavior and improve our site. This automated collection includes:

  • Page Views: Which specific pages you visit and how long you stay there.
  • Scrolls: How far down a page you scroll (measured automatically at 90% depth).
  • Product & Portfolio Views: Which work/portfolio items you click on to view details.
  • Traffic Sources: Whether you found our site via search engines, social media, or by typing our URL directly.
  • Device & Tech Data: Whether you are visiting from a smartphone, tablet, or desktop computer, along with your browser type and operating system.
  • Geographic Location: The general country and city where you are located.

Google Analytics uses cookies (small text files placed on your device) to collect this aggregate data. You can configure your browser settings to disable cookies if you prefer not to participate in this tracking.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected exclusively for professional consultation, client communication, and website optimization. Specifically, to:

  • Respond to your hardware design queries and project consultation requests.
  • Provide project quotes, schedule meetings, and follow up on business inquiries.
  • Comply with necessary engineering NDA standards and legal verification procedures.
  • Analyze aggregated visitor trends to improve our website design, usability, and performance.

4. Data Security and Protection

We implement standard industry security practices to safeguard your data against unauthorized access, loss, or disclosure. Because our website functions as a secure informational portfolio and does not collect credentials, we do not host user accounts, passwords, or credit card databases directly on this site, greatly minimizing potential security risks.

5. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or distribute your personal details to third-party marketing companies under any circumstances. We only share information with Google Analytics for performance analysis, with partners when necessary to fulfill your hardware project requirements (with your explicit, prior consent), or if legally obligated by law.

6. Your Rights and Deletion

You have the right to request access to the personal information we hold about you (from our email communications) or request that we delete your contact records from our communication databases. If you wish to make a request, please contact us directly at our email address.

7. Contact Information

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or our data handling practices, please feel free to reach out to us via our Contact Page.
