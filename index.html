Project Origin and Motivation

Levi Puritch, the Co-Founder and CEO of LTRJ Labs is a volunteer fire fighter at Willis Point Volunteer Fire Dept. MothNode’s development began in May of 2026, following an ongoing issue with the department’s water tanks in Willis Point being brought to his attention. Their main issues were tanks leaking only up to a certain level, then stopping, as well as tanks struggling to stay maintained due to crews only dedicating 2 hours a week for maintaining all the department’s equipment, alongside training.

The use of these water tanks is critical in this industry for effective firefighting. A good benchmark of reliability and function for these tanks is to compare it to a regular fire hydrant in an urban area. What is expected of it? A short list being, near infinite supply of water, any department can use them, uniform interface, and it’s location to be known and documented. Fire Protection Infrastructure specifically qualifies new functionality to be brought to the table for tank monitoring for this reason.
