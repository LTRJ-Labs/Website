Our Story

The journey of LTRJ Labs

LTRJ Labs began with a simple, shared realization: the world of hardware is changing rapidly, and bringing an idea to life requires more than just standard templates—it requires relentless execution.

Founded in Victoria BC, we are a small group of ambitious electronics enthusiasts and entrepreneurs ready to start our journey in electronics design, and we are willing to put in the work to make a lasting impact in this industry.

For us, this isn't just theory. We wanted a way to take the design principles and testing methods we were mastering in our labs and classes and immediately apply them to practical hardware challenges. What started as pushing our own lab projects to the next level quickly turned into a business. We realized that by combining our strengths in hardware layout, microcontroller programming, and prototyping, we could help local businesses and makers get their ideas off the ground and working reliably.

At LTRJ Labs, we don't just engineer boards; we bridge the gap between a conceptual circuit diagram and a robust, functional reality. We tackle every project with the discipline of modern engineering technology and the drive of hungry entrepreneurs. We're ready to design the future—one layer at a time.
